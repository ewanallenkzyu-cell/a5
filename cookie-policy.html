Digital Privacy

Institutional Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: September 15, 2026

1. Understanding Digital Cookies & Tracking

Cookies are compact text data files placed upon your computer, mobile workstation, or tablet browser by web servers when you navigate digital domains. At FacultyBright, operating from our headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, we utilize cookies and analogous web technologies to authenticate scholar sessions, preserve user display preferences, and aggregate anonymized visitor telemetry across our higher education platform.

2. Essential & Functional Session Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly mandatory to ensure the core operational functionality, cryptographic security, and accessibility of our educational portal. These cookies enable registered scholars to navigate secure fellowship portals, access password-protected syllabus archives, and submit workshop evaluation dossiers securely without session interruption. Because our platform cannot function securely without these essential files, they cannot be deactivated within our administrative preference settings or browser controls without breaking core site functionality.

3. Performance & Scholarly Analytics Telemetry

We deploy performance and analytics telemetry, notably Google Analytics Tag G-0LY0HY7L01, to gather anonymized aggregate data regarding visitor traffic density, monograph reading durations, and link navigation pathways. This telemetry assists our provost council in assessing which pedagogical treatises and program descriptions resonate with university communities, enabling continuous technical optimization of our web infrastructure without tracking individual personal identities, proprietary research notes, or student classroom interactions.

4. Personalization & Preference Preservation

Functional personalization cookies allow our web servers to remember your selected preferences between academic visits, including font accessibility scalings, language regionalizations, and previously expanded accordion segments on our FAQ modules. By preserving these customized states, our educational portal delivers an efficient, uninterrupted scholarly research experience tailored to your specific desktop or mobile device configuration across multiple campus research sessions and visiting faculty logins.

5. Scholar Control & Browser Management Options

University scholars maintain full autonomous authority to inspect, accept, decline, or systematically erase digital cookies through standard configuration settings built into all modern web browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Please be advised that disabling certain functional or session cookies may limit your ability to access secure fellow registries, interactive seminar features, or customized syllabus tools across our domain.

6. Institutional Governance & Technical Queries

For questions or technical clarifications regarding our deployment of cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tags, university administrators and individual scholars are invited to contact the FacultyBright Technical Secretariat in writing at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by telephoning our institutional operations desk at +1-888-777-5845 during weekday operating hours. Our technical team is available to assist with browser security configurations and data protection compliance.
