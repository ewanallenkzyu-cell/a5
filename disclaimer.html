Compliance Notice

Institutional Disclaimer & Disclosures

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: September 15, 2026

1. General Educational Information Standard

All academic analyses, pedagogical methodologies, grant writing guidance, and leadership frameworks presented across the FacultyBright website and within our fellowship colloquia at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are published strictly for general educational and professional development purposes. While our curriculum is informed by credentialed provosts and national grant panelists, our materials do not constitute binding legal, fiscal, or formal accreditation guarantees for university tenure dossiers.

2. External Grant Funding Disclosures

Participation in our STEM Grant Writing Accelerator or peer proposal labs does not guarantee monetary awards, funding allocations, or positive study section scores from external funding bodies such as the National Science Foundation, National Institutes of Health, or private philanthropic foundations. Award determinations remain within the sovereign discretion of the respective granting agencies, their independent peer review panels, and federal appropriations guidelines beyond our institutional control.

3. Independent Institutional Tenure Adjudication

Faculty tenure track milestones, promotion criteria, sabbatical authorizations, and merit salary evaluations are established independently by each candidate's home academic institution, faculty senate bylaws, and collective bargaining agreements. FacultyBright maintains no authority over, nor responsibility for, individual tenure review outcomes, and our certificates of pedagogical completion represent voluntary professional development achievements rather than mandatory statutory qualifications, accreditation endorsements, or guaranteed promotional appointments at any collegiate level.

4. Third-Party Academic Links & Citations

Our scholarly monographs, treatises, and syllabus repositories may contain hyperlinks to external university libraries, government research repositories, academic presses, and digital indexing platforms operated by independent third parties. FacultyBright does not endorse, control, verify, or assume legal liability for the content, privacy practices, accessibility, or accuracy of information maintained on external web servers outside the facultybright.com administrative domain, and users access such external resources at their own academic discretion.

5. Continuous Curricular Evolution & Revisions

The landscape of higher education pedagogy, research compliance, and academic technology evolves continuously. Consequently, FacultyBright reserves the right to update, modify, retire, or augment any portion of our course syllabi, diagnostic rubrics, and published guidelines without preliminary public notice. We make no warranty that our digital materials remain perpetually free from typographical errors, technical oversights, or evolving regulatory modifications, and scholars are advised to verify currency regularly.

6. Regulatory Compliance & Support Vector

If you require clarification regarding any disclosure, institutional policy, or disclaimer articulated within this document, please communicate with our Office of Academic Affairs in writing at FacultyBright, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact our central academic support secretariat via our dedicated toll-free phone line at +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our administrative provost council remains committed to resolving all institutional inquiries promptly and transparently.
