Terms and Conditions of Academic Fellowship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: September 15, 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms & Governance

By registering for, accessing, or participating in academic programs, provost colloquia, faculty development tracks, or digital seminar archives administered by FacultyBright from its headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, you unequivocally agree to be legally bound by these comprehensive Terms and Conditions. If you do not consent to every provision articulated herein, you must immediately abstain from enrolling in our academic tracks.

2. Intellectual Property & Research Autonomy

All curriculum syllabi, pedagogical rubrics, diagnostic benchmarks, and proprietary workshop publications developed by FacultyBright remain the protected intellectual property of the Institute and are licensed exclusively for authorized educational use. Conversely, all grant proposals, unpublished laboratory datasets, scholarly treatises, and pedagogical adaptations produced by enrolled fellows remain the absolute, unencumbered intellectual property of the individual scholar and their sponsoring home institution.

3. Academic Integrity & Professional Decorum

Fellows and participating faculty members are required to maintain the highest standards of intellectual honesty, collegial ethics, and peer respect throughout all colloquia, breakout laboratories, and digital feedback exchanges. Any documented instance of scientific falsification, academic plagiarism, unauthorized distribution of confidential peer research drafts, or disruptive unprofessional misconduct will result in immediate termination of fellowship credentials without monetary tuition reimbursement.

4. Institutional Sponsoring & Tuition Policies

Enrollment fees, institutional provost sponsorship retainers, and executive seminar dues must be remitted in full prior to the designated cohort launch date in accordance with the official invoice schedule issued by our admissions office. Tuition refunds are available exclusively upon receipt of formal written withdrawal notification submitted at least twenty business days before program commencement, subject to an administrative processing deduction of fifteen percent.

5. Limitation of Liability & Educational Warranty

FacultyBright provides structured pedagogical frameworks and expert peer mentorship designed to elevate scholarly efficacy; however, the Institute does not guarantee specific tenure committee adjudications, external grant awards, or peer-reviewed publication approvals. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, FacultyBright disclaims all liability for consequential, incidental, or indirect career damages resulting from the application or interpretation of our academic training curricula.

Any legal disputes arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts situated in New York County.
